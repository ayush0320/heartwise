Disclaimer and privacy

Please read this before you use {{ cfg.PROJECT_TITLE }}.

Not medical advice

{{ cfg.PROJECT_TITLE }} is a student project for education. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or rule out any condition. The estimates come from a model trained on 303 patients from one hospital. In testing on other hospitals' data the model missed many patients who had disease. Always talk to a qualified doctor about your health.

In an emergency

For severe chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting or any sign of a heart attack, call your local emergency number straight away (112 in India). Do not wait for a result from this site.

What is stored

Assessments, patient records and readings you enter are saved in this deployment's own database. There are no accounts. Anyone who can open this site can see everything in it. Nothing is sent to outside services. PDF and CSV files are generated on request and not kept.

If this site is public, enter made-up details only. Do not enter real names, real health data or anything you would not want others to see. Demo patients on this site are fictional and built from public research records.

Data and limits

Training data: UCI Heart Disease dataset (Cleveland subset). Validation data: Hungarian, Switzerland and VA Long Beach subsets. These records are decades old, come from four institutions and include few women, so the model may not suit other populations.

Security notes for deployers

This app has no user login, by design of this version. Before real use, put it behind authentication, use HTTPS, and set READ_ONLY_ADMIN=1 on public deployments so strangers cannot retrain the model or create API keys.
