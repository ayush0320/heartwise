Standard threshold: {{ (thresholds.standard * 100)|round|int }}%

A patient is flagged when the estimate is at least this high. It keeps false alarms low, but on other hospitals' data it misses roughly half of the patients with disease.

Screening threshold: {{ (thresholds.screening * 100)|round|int }}%

Chosen automatically as the highest threshold that still catches 90% of true cases in cross-validated predictions on the training data. It raises recall everywhere, and costs precision.

How the model was built

  • Data: UCI Heart Disease, Cleveland subset ({{ m.n_total }} patients), 13 inputs. Source: {{ m.source }}.
  • Cleaning: "?" and impossible zeros (blood pressure, cholesterol) become missing; the 0-4 severity label becomes disease / no disease; missing values are filled with the training median.
  • Split: stratified 80/20 ({{ m.n_train }} / {{ m.n_test }}), fixed random seed.
  • Model selection: logistic regression, decision tree, random forest and XGBoost compared with cross-validation.
  • Explainability: SHAP TreeExplainer, values in percentage points.
  • Validation: the frozen model is scored on the Hungarian, Switzerland and VA Long Beach files from the same UCI repository.
  • Screening threshold: highest threshold with at least 90% recall on cross-validated training predictions.
