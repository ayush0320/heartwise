Train a new version

Upload a CSV with the 13 input columns and a target column (0/1, or 0-4 severity). At least 100 rows with 20 or more of each class. Training takes under a minute and the new version is not activated automatically.

Tip: to test the flow, export a few hundred rows of the UCI files with the same columns.

How to decide

  • Prefer the version with the better held-out ROC-AUC and recall, but check the sample sizes: tiny test sets are noisy.
  • Look at the Model page after activating, especially the other-hospital results.
  • Old assessments keep the version they were made with, so history stays honest.
