When to get help straight away

Call your local emergency number (112 in India) for chest pain that is severe, lasts more than a few minutes or spreads to the arm, jaw or back, especially with breathlessness, sweating, nausea or fainting. Do not use this website to decide whether to seek help.

What you can and cannot change

You can influence

  • Blood pressure: less salt, more activity, healthy weight, limited alcohol
  • Cholesterol: more vegetables, fruit, whole grains and pulses; less fried and processed food
  • Blood sugar: weight, activity and diet, with regular checks
  • Smoking and tobacco: stopping is one of the biggest single steps
  • Physical activity: about 150 minutes a week of moderate activity is a common recommendation, if your doctor agrees

You cannot change, but should know

  • Age and sex
  • Family history of early heart disease
  • Test results such as ECG patterns and imaging findings. They are for a cardiologist to interpret and follow up

Questions people ask

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It is an educational screening aid trained on 303 patients from one hospital. Only a doctor can diagnose heart disease.

What does "flagged" mean?

The estimate is at or above the threshold of the chosen mode (50% in standard mode). It means "worth a closer look by a doctor", not "you have heart disease".

What is screening mode?

It lowers the threshold so the tool misses fewer patients with disease, at the price of more false alarms. We added it because, on other hospitals' data, the standard threshold missed about half of the patients with disease.

What is SHAP?

A method that shares the credit for one prediction among the inputs. Starting from the average patient, each input adds or removes some percentage points, and the pieces add up exactly to the estimate. It shows what the model relied on, not what causes disease.

Where does the data come from?

The UCI Machine Learning Repository's Heart Disease dataset: Cleveland, Hungarian, Switzerland and VA Long Beach files, collected by cardiology centres in the 1980s. The model is trained on Cleveland only.

Is my data stored?

Assessments are saved in this site's own database so you can see history and trends. Nothing is sent to third parties. If this is a public demo, use made-up details. See the privacy notes.
