How it works

Enter measurements

Age, blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG results and a few more. Nothing needs a lab you can't get at a normal check-up.

Get an estimate and the reason

A Random Forest trained on the UCI Cleveland data scores the patient. SHAP explains each input's share.
